⚠️ Key Risks

AI Accuracy Unvalidated — could be 30% or 70%
Cash Cycle 60-90 day PSA turnaround = capital tied up
Market Risk Card values can drop during grading
Grade Variance Same card can get 9 or 10 on resubmit
Operational Shipping damage, lost cards, disputes
Reality Check: This is not a SaaS business — it's a trading operation. Different risk profile, lower multiples (2-4x vs 6-10x for SaaS). Requires working capital facility.
